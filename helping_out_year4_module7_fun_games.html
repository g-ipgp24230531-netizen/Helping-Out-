🎒 Whose Is It?

Use possessive pronouns to say who owns something.

🎒
Whose bag is this?

It is my bag. It is MINE.

👦Ali's pencil

It is his.

👧Mia's ruler

It is hers.

👫Our books

They are ours.

💡 Remember: mine = belongs to me; yours = belongs to you; his = belongs to him; hers = belongs to her; ours = belongs to us; theirs = belongs to them.

📚 User Guide

Simple instructions for pupils and teachers.

🧒 For pupils

  1. Choose Learn, Whose is it? or Recycle to study.
  2. Press the speaker buttons to listen to words and sentences.
  3. Open Game Zone and choose one of the four games.
  4. Read each instruction and complete the activity.
  5. Collect stars and try again to improve your score!

👩‍🏫 For teachers

  1. Open this HTML file in an updated browser such as Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
  2. Use a projector, interactive whiteboard or pupils' devices.
  3. Introduce the learning content before pupils play the games.
  4. Encourage pupils to answer in complete sentences and explain their choices.
  5. Preview the external video and check the classroom internet connection.

🔐 Ethical use
